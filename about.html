Our Story

Building the Future of Industry

For over 27 years, INDUSTRIX has been at the forefront of industrial services and manufacturing. What started as a small fabrication shop in Houston has grown into one of the most trusted names in heavy industry.

Where It All Began

From Workshop to Industry Leader

Founded in 1997 by engineer Robert Kessler, INDUSTRIX began as a three-person operation in a 5,000 sq ft shop on Houston's east side. Robert's vision was simple: deliver fabrication work so precise and reliable that clients would never need to look elsewhere.

That philosophy held. By 2005, INDUSTRIX had expanded to a 120,000 sq ft facility with 60 employees. By 2015, we had opened satellite offices in Dallas, Denver, and Calgary. Today, with over 120 engineers and a fleet of advanced CNC and fabrication equipment, INDUSTRIX serves clients across energy, mining, aerospace, and infrastructure.

Through every phase of growth, the mission has remained unchanged: engineering excellence, built to last.

Our Core Values

Precision

Every measurement matters. We hold tolerances that others consider impossible, because in our industry, "close enough" is never good enough.

Craftsmanship

We take pride in the work itself. Clean welds, precise fits, and finishes that reflect the expertise of the people who made them.
