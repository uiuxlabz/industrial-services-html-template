Our Capabilities

Comprehensive Industrial Solutions

From concept through completion, INDUSTRIX provides end-to-end industrial services backed by 27 years of engineering expertise and an unwavering commitment to quality.

Core Service

Heavy Fabrication

Our fabrication division operates from a 120,000 sq ft facility equipped with overhead cranes rated to 50 tons, CNC plasma and oxy-fuel cutting tables, and a full suite of forming and rolling equipment. We handle projects from single-component repairs to full structural assemblies.

  • Structural steel for buildings, bridges, and platforms
  • Pressure vessels and tanks to ASME standards
  • Custom machinery frames and bases
  • Material handling equipment and chutes
  • Prototype and short-run production
Maintenance

Plant Maintenance

Downtime costs more than repairs. Our maintenance programs are designed to prevent failures before they happen, using predictive analytics and scheduled interventions that keep your operation running at peak performance.

  • Preventive maintenance scheduling and execution
  • Emergency breakdown response (24/7 availability)
  • Equipment condition assessment and reporting
  • Turnaround planning and management
  • Corrosion monitoring and mitigation
Precision

Precision Machining

Our CNC shop houses 5-axis machining centers, CNC lathes with live tooling, and wire EDM equipment capable of holding tolerances within 2 microns. We serve industries where failure is not an option.

  • 5-axis CNC milling and turning
  • Wire and sinker EDM machining
  • Surface and cylindrical grinding
  • In-house CMM inspection and metrology
  • Materials: steel, stainless, aluminum, titanium, Inconel
Certified

Industrial Welding

Our welding department maintains certifications to AWS D1.1, ASME Section IX, and API 1104 standards. We employ certified welders across all major processes and materials, supported by a full-time welding engineer.

  • MIG, TIG, stick, and submerged arc welding
  • Alloy and exotic material welding (Inconel, Hastelloy)
  • On-site field welding and repair
  • Welding procedure qualification (WPS/PQR)
  • Non-destructive testing (UT, MT, RT, PT)
How We Work

Our Process

A proven four-phase approach that transforms your requirements into precision-engineered reality.

01

Consultation

We begin with a thorough assessment of your requirements, site conditions, and performance goals. Our engineers collaborate with your team to define scope and specifications.

02

Engineering

Detailed design and engineering using advanced CAD/CAM software. FEA analysis, material selection, and fabrication planning ensure optimal results before production begins.

03

Execution

Precision manufacturing and fabrication carried out by certified professionals under strict quality control. Real-time progress reporting keeps you informed at every stage.

04

Delivery

Final inspection, testing, and documentation before delivery. We provide comprehensive as-built records, maintenance guides, and ongoing support after handover.
